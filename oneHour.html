One Hour Programming

Concept

In an effort to consolidate my programming skills, I set a challenge for myself, to semi-regularly complete programming projects in as close to one hour as possible. These projects are extremely small scale and a little silly, in order to push me to create something functional rather than perfected. Although they can look a little rough around the edges, overall I love each of these projects, and the creative process behind them. Despite the point being functionality rather than aesthetic, after the hour is up, I'll take a little extra time to polish it up (as a treat for me).
